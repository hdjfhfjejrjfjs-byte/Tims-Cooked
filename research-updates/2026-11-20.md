## Later static checkpoint — 2026-11-20 (pager-ready producer census)

- Enumerated every kernel store of a word at the candidate virtual-memory-object flags offset that sits near an `#0x200` logical operation: 59 sites per build, zero atomic read-modify-write sites.
- Identified the two virtual-memory-object writers of the “pager ready” bit: the pager-association routine behind the freezer/pageout chain (sets the bit only when the object’s internal flag is set, with companion state changes) and a memory-object attribute handler whose callers are the vnode/device/shared-region/dyld/swapfile/apple-protect pager initialisers.
- Corrected the association-producer identity recorded in earlier local notes: it is the routine called from the freezer pageout wrapper, not the attribute handler.
- Classified four previously unclassified candidate stores as VFS/vnode, network-interface, packet-buffer and Skywalk host-networking state; matching offsets and bit numbers alone do not establish object identity.
- No new app-triggerable “pager ready” route was found, and no device run was possible this cycle (no authorised Mac enumerated a device). No vulnerability, kernel read/write primitive, or jailbreak is claimed.
